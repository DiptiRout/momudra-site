Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 July 2026

MoMudra ("the app") is developed by Diptiranjan Rout ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives, and what leaves your device. The short version: your financial data belongs to you and stays with you.

1. Your financial data stays on your device

Transactions, budgets, income, saving goals, and recurring expenses are stored locally on your iPhone. We operate no user accounts and keep no copy of your financial records on our servers.

2. iCloud backup & sync (Pro, optional)

If you enable iCloud Backup & Sync (a MoMudra Pro feature), your data is stored in your personal, private iCloud database managed by Apple and encrypted in transit and at rest. We cannot read, access, or recover it — only your Apple ID can. You can turn sync off at any time in Profile → Data & Backup.

3. AI features

When you use an AI feature (AI logging, Ask AI, Spending Story, insights), the text you type or dictate and a compact numeric summary of your spending needed to answer (for example category totals or your remaining budget) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards them to an AI provider (such as Anthropic or Groq) solely to generate the response. This data is:

  • used only to produce your answer — never for advertising;
  • not sold or shared with data brokers;
  • not stored by us after the response is returned.

Voice input is transcribed on your device by Apple's speech recognition; audio never reaches our servers. If you never use an AI feature, no spending information leaves your device (except via your own iCloud sync, if enabled).

4. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which features are used and to protect the service from abuse, the app sends anonymous usage events to our server. Each event contains only: an event name (e.g. "AI parse succeeded"), a random install identifier generated on first launch, the app version, build channel, platform, and a timestamp.

Analytics never include transaction contents, amounts, merchant names, notes, your name, email, contacts, precise location, or any advertising identifier. The random install ID is not linked to your identity, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Sign in with Apple (Pro, optional)

If you use Sign in with Apple, the name and email Apple shares are stored only on your device (in the system keychain) to personalize the app. We do not transmit them to our servers.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

7. Data deletion

You can erase everything at any time: Profile → Delete all data removes all app data from your device. If iCloud sync was enabled, you can also remove the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → MoMudra.

8. Children

MoMudra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.

10. Contact
