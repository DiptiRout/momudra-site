Common questions

How do I log a transaction fast?

Tap the big + button (or the Quick Log widget). Pro users can speak or type naturally — "500 dinner at Olive" — and AI fills everything in.

How do recurring expenses work?

Plans → Recurring. Add bills once; each pay cycle they show as due, and tapping the checkbox marks them paid (logging the real expense). MoMudra also detects repeating patterns and suggests tracking them.

My subscription isn't showing as active.

Open Profile → MoMudra Pro → Restore Purchases, and make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID that bought it.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

MoMudra Pro's iCloud Backup & Sync restores your data automatically on any device signed into your iCloud. Anyone can also export a CSV from Profile → Data & Backup.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete all data removes everything from the device. iCloud data (if sync was on) can be removed in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → MoMudra.
