Know your money.

MoMudra is an AI-powered personal finance app for iPhone. Log spending in seconds — by voice, text, or a tap — and always know what's truly yours to spend this pay cycle.

Private by design. Your financial data lives on your device (and, with Pro, in your own private iCloud). We run no accounts and store none of your finances on our servers.
